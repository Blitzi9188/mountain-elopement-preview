Where to begin

Blend adventure
and romance

Curious about designing an elopement that seamlessly blends adventure and romance in the breathtaking Dolomites? Our expertise lies in curating unforgettable mountain elopements tailored to your unique vision and preferences.

We begin by helping you select the perfect mountain location — considering accessibility, scenery, and the mood you desire. Whether you dream of exchanging vows on a secluded peak or beside a tranquil alpine lake, every detail is shaped around the two of you.

When the day calls for more hands, we work with a trusted circle: on-the-ground planning by Dolomites Wedding Planner, photography by Blitzkneisser and cinematic coverage by No Matter The Weather.

What to consider

STEP 01

Choose the location

Peak, lake, meadow or ridge — we match the setting to your vision, the season, and how far you want to walk.

STEP 02

Plan the day

A relaxed timeline, the best light, a flexible weather plan, and every logistic handled — transfers, flowers, hair & make-up.

STEP 03

Say your vows

Personal vows, an optional celebrant or civil ceremony, and photography that captures it exactly as it felt.
