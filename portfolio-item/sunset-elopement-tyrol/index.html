Mountain-Top Sunset Elopement

Elopement · Mountain

The other golden hour — warm Tyrolean light falling long across the ridges.

Where sunrise is cool and solitary, sunset is warm and unhurried — a whole evening softening toward gold. In Tyrol the couple walked up in the afternoon and let the day burn down slowly around them, no alarm and no rush.

Sunset suits couples who’d rather not rise in the dark — the same big light, at a far kinder hour.
