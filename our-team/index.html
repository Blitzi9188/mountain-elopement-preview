The core team

Jlenia & Andreas

The small local team behind your day.

We’re a small team, at home in the Dolomites — planner Jlenia and photographer Andreas. But ‘team’ doesn’t quite cover it: we’re closest friends first, our families share weekends and our kids grow up side by side. That friendship shows on the day. When the two people planning and photographing your elopement truly trust each other and read each other without a word, everything runs softer — no friction, no awkward hand-offs, just two people who’ve done this together for years, moving as one.

The mountains are where we feel most ourselves — still summits before the first hikers, hidden lakes that hold the dawn light for twelve minutes. We know these places not from a list but because we’ve stood there, season after season, in every weather. We plan and photograph your whole day, start to finish, and bring in a trusted circle where it helps — flowers, film, ceremony, cake. But you only ever deal with us. One team, one point of contact, two friends who happen to be very good at this.
