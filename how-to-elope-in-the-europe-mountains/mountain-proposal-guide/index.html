How to Plan a Mountain Proposal

A proposal in the mountains is equal parts logistics and nerve. Here is how we help you choose the moment, keep it a secret, and photograph it without your partner ever noticing us.

At a glance
Best season
Year-round
Best light
Sunrise
Lead time
2–8 weeks

Choosing the moment

First light is our favourite: quiet trails, soft colour and almost no one around. We scout a spot with a natural pause — a summit, a viewpoint, a lakeshore — where kneeling feels effortless.

Keeping it a secret

We plan everything by message, shoot from a distance with a long lens, and pass for ordinary hikers until the yes. Afterwards we stay for a proper couples session to celebrate.

And after the yes?

We keep shooting for a relaxed couples session, then send you a few images the same day to share with family.

Good to know

  • Pick a spot with a natural reason to pause.
  • Keep the ring somewhere secure on the walk.
  • Tell us the signal so we're ready.
