Pizza Elopement at Tre Cime

Dolomites · Elopement · Mountain

Vows at the Cadini di Misurina, near the Tre Cime — and a celebratory pizza at the top to seal it.

The Cadini di Misurina are a crowd of slender spires beside the famous Tre Cime di Lavaredo — one of the great sights of the Dolomites. Up here the couple traded formality for joy, saying their vows on the rock and then splitting a proper Italian pizza with the peaks for a table.

That is the spirit we love — a day that is epic and a little bit fun, tradition worn lightly. Bring an appetite; the view is free.
