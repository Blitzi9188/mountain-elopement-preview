Elopement · Mountain

Legally married on the mountain — a real registrar, a real certificate, at the top of the Alps.

In Austria your wedding on the summit can be the legal one — a registrar climbs up with you and the marriage you sign for is binding, not symbolic. No paperwork waiting at home; the real thing, thin air and all.

We arrange the registrar, the permits and the timing, so the only thing you carry up the mountain is the moment itself.
