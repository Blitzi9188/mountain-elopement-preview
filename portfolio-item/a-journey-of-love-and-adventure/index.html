A Journey of Love on Top of Innsbruck

Couple · Engagement

An elopement built like an expedition — boots laced before dawn, vows earned one switchback at a time.

Some couples don’t want a venue; they want a journey. This day began at the trailhead and climbed with the sun, trading crowds and schedules for ridgelines, wind and the simple company of each other.

We kept the pace unhurried and the plan loose, letting the mountain set the rhythm. What you see here is the whole arc — the walk up, the quiet promise at the top, and the golden descent home.
