The core team

Jlenia, Andreas & Stefanie

Mountain Elopement — a small local team behind your day.

We are a small, local team at home in the Dolomites and the Alps — planner Jlenia, photographer Andreas and filmmaker Stefanie. For years we have guided couples to quiet summits and hidden lakes, capturing the day exactly as it feels — unposed, unhurried, real.

Between us we photograph, film and plan your entire day — and where it makes your day even better, we bring in trusted partners like hair & make-up, below.
