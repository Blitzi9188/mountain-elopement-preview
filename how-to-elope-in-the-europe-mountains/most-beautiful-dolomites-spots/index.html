The Most Beautiful Places in the Dolomites

After years of shooting here, a handful of places keep drawing us back — each with its own light, mood and effort. Here are the ones we love most, and how to choose between them.

At a glance

Lago di Braies & the great lakes

Emerald water, the old boathouse and peaks rising straight from the shore. Braies is iconic — and at dawn, before the boats, wonderfully quiet.

Seceda, Tre Cime & the high ridges

For sheer drama, nothing beats the jagged ridgelines. Some are a short cable-car ride away, others a proper hike — we match the spot to how far you want to walk.

Hidden lakes for the adventurous

Away from the icons, quieter water rewards a walk. Lago di Sorapis glows an unreal milky turquoise a couple of hours from Passo Tre Croci; Lago di Federa mirrors the Croda da Lago inside a ring of larches; little Lago di Limides catches the Tofane just off Passo Falzarego; and roadside Lago d'Antorno frames the Cadini spires with no effort at all. These are the spots we send couples who want Braies' beauty without Braies' crowds — and they photograph beautifully in any weather, as this day near the Tre Cime shows.

Passes, meadows & the Cinque Torri

For big scenery you can almost drive to, the high passes are hard to beat. Passo Giau is a meadow amphitheatre ringed by peaks; Cinque Torri — five tower-like rocks reached by a short chairlift from Bai de Dones — give an intimate stage with a First-World-War history; and the Averau and Nuvolau refuges above them serve dinner with a 360° view. These roadside and lift-served spots are perfect when you'd rather save your legs for dancing — and glorious for both sunrise and sunset.

How to reach each one

Access decides your morning. Braies limits summer traffic to the lake from roughly 10:00 to 16:00, so we go before it; the Tre Cime toll road (about €30–45 per car) drives you almost to the base; Seceda, Lagazuoi and Faloria are cable cars; Sorapis and Federa are hikes with no vehicle access. We plan the route, the tickets and the timing around all of it — the full logistics live in how to plan your elopement.

How to choose your spot

We match the place to your fitness, the season and the mood you want — lakeside calm, ridge-top drama or a quiet meadow.

Good to know

  • Go at first light to beat the crowds.
  • Emerald lakes glow brightest in the morning.
  • Ask us about permit-free spots.
