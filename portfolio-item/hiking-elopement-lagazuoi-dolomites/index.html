Hiking Elopement at Lagazuoi, Dolomites

Dolomites · Elopement · Mountain

Lagazuoi — a cable car, a mountain refuge at 2,750 m, and a balcony over half the Dolomites.

From the Falzarego Pass a cable car climbs to the Rifugio Lagazuoi, where the terrace looks straight across to the Tofane and the Marmolada. The peak still carries its First World War tunnels; today it carries couples who want the biggest view for the smallest wedding.

You can arrive on foot or by cable car, sleep at the refuge, and wake above the clouds for first light. It is adventure with a warm bed at the end of it.
