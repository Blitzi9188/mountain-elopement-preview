Our Mission

Crafting your
perfect elopement

to elope — to slip away from the ordinary and marry where the world falls silent.

We design intimate weddings that reflect your unique love story. If you want to skip the extravagance of traditional venues and long guest lists, you have found your partner. We celebrate individuality while weaving timeless romance into every elopement we create.

From crystal-clear lakes to snow-capped peaks and tranquil meadows, our handpicked locations across Europe are chosen to match your vision — and we guide you every step of the way.

What makes us different

Full-service, truly full

Planning, permits, flowers, cake, ceremony, photography and film — one local team, one point of contact. You arrive, we have taken care of everything else.

Epic without the climb

From helicopter elopements above the Dolomites to hand-picked locations reachable by cable car: we create breathtaking days for couples who want the summit feeling — with or without the six-hour hike.

Legally married, right on the mountain

As Austrians, we know how to make your mountain wedding legally binding — with a registrar at the summit. Real ceremony, real certificate, real mountains. (Symbolic ceremonies in the Italian Dolomites, of course, are just as beautiful.)

Born here, at home here

A small, local team at home between Innsbruck and the Dolomites — planner Jlenia, photographer Andreas and filmmaker Stefanie. Award-winning: Way Up North Awards 2024.
